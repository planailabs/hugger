Why archive models?

The model you build on today may not be downloadable tomorrow. Archiving turns “hosted somewhere” into “owned by you.”

⚖️

Regulation

New AI rules and export controls can force models to be geofenced, gated, or pulled. A local archive keeps you working regardless of where policy lands.

🗑️

Takedowns & relicensing

Repos get deleted, made private, or switched to restrictive licenses. If you didn’t download it, you may never get that exact version again.

🔁

Reproducibility

Research and audits need the exact commit. hugger pins the revision sha so results stay reproducible years later.

📡

Offline & sovereignty

Air-gapped labs, spotty networks, and data-residency rules all need models stored on infrastructure you control.

How it works

Run the small self-hosted hugger server, point the extension at it, and archive from any model page.

Self-host the server. A tiny Python app (web UI + API) that downloads full model snapshots to disk. Internet-exposable, with login + token auth, or a NixOS module for one-line deploys.
Install the extension & paste your token. Chrome, Firefox (desktop + Android), and Edge. The background worker holds the token; the page never sees it.
Archive in one click. On any huggingface.co model page, hit ⤓ Archive. hugger downloads the snapshot and tracks the commit.
Manage & update. The button shows ✓ Archived with Remove, and offers Update when a newer revision is published.
